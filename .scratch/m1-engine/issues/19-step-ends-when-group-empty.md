# 19: A step ends when its process group is empty

Status: ready-for-agent
Category: bug
Type: task
Blocked by: 18

## What

A step is over only when its whole process group is empty, not when its leader exits (ADR-0010). qwe becomes a child subreaper, so that orphans in a step's group are reparented to it, and it sends `group-empty` once no process of the group is left. Survivors of the leader get the normal teardown with the full grace period.

This fixes two bugs that exist today:
- **Processes left behind:** `run: sleep 4242 & echo done` succeeds, and the `sleep` outlives qwe.
- **Grace period cut short:** when a torn-down step's leader exits, the rest of its group is SIGKILLed at once, so a child that traps SIGTERM loses the remainder of its grace period.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A step that leaves a background child and exits 0 is `success`. The child gets SIGTERM, qwe waits for the group to empty, and no process is left behind. The job's trace shows `settling-continue-term` before `group-empty`. `e2e: tests/e2e/straggler_after_success/`
- [ ] A step whose child traps SIGTERM keeps its full grace period after the leader exits, and is SIGKILLed only when grace expires. The trace shows `grace-expired`, then `settling-*-kill`, then `group-empty`. `e2e: tests/e2e/grace_outlives_leader/` (with `QWE_TEST_GRACE_MS`)
- [ ] qwe is a child subreaper for the whole run and reaps every process reparented to it, not only step leaders. `unit: src/kernel/proc_test.c::subreaper_reaps_orphans`
- [ ] A process that escapes with `setsid` is not waited on, and it is noted as the known gap (design §9.3). No test is expected to catch it: that is the cgroup ticket.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Make "the process group is empty", not "the leader exited", the end of every step, so that no step leaves a process behind and stragglers get the full grace period.

**Current behavior:**
After ticket 18, the shell sends `group-empty` straight after reaping the leader. Background children of a successful step keep running after qwe exits. After a teardown, the group is SIGKILLed as soon as the leader exits.

**Desired behavior:**
qwe sets itself as child subreaper at the start of a run. On every SIGCHLD it reaps whatever has exited. A step's group counts as empty when no live process has that process group id; how that is detected is up to the implementer (reaping by pgid, or scanning). Only then does it send `group-empty` for that step. After a leader exits, any survivors get SIGTERM and the grace timer through the table's `settling-*` transitions. They are never killed at once.

**Key interfaces:**
- The process module gains "make this process a child subreaper", and "is group G empty?" (or reaping by group).
- The shell sends `group-empty` with the step index.

**Acceptance criteria:** the checklist in this ticket's Acceptance criteria section.

**Out of scope:**
- cgroups, and processes that escape with `setsid` (step-containment ticket 01).
- Any change to the table from ticket 17 beyond fixing a cell this ticket proves wrong. If a cell does change, the ADR and the layer-2 rules must agree with it.
